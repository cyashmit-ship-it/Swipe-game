# Is It a Match? (working title)

A Tinder-style food swipe game for market research at the MDI fest.

- Part 1: five Indian dishes. Would you try a Chinese twist?
- Part 2: five world cuisines. Would you try them with an Indian twist?
- Answers: Yes or No. In part 2 each player also gets one movable Desi Crush (saved as "Super Like"). Undo works for the last swipe only.
- Every swipe is saved straight to a Google Sheet.

Edit text, dishes and photo names in `data.js`. Setup steps are in `SETUP.md`.
