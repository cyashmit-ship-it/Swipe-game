# Is It a Match? (working title)

A Tinder-style food swipe game for market research at the MDI fest.

- Round 1: five Indian dishes. Would you try it Chinese-style? A Yes reveals the twist (Schezwan Dosa, Noodle Samosa…).
- Round 2: five world cuisines. Would you try it desi-style? A Yes reveals two desi dishes.
- Answers: Yes or No. In round 2 each player also gets one movable Desi Crush (saved as "Super Like"). Undo works for the last swipe only.
- About a minute per player: one round switch; the match screen moves on by itself; No has no screen.
- Tokens are numbers only (07 = 7). A token being played on one device can't be opened on another.
- The end screen gives a random "perfect match" dish, just for fun (not saved).
- Every swipe is saved straight to a Google Sheet.

Edit text, dishes and photo names in `data.js`. Setup steps are in `SETUP.md`.
