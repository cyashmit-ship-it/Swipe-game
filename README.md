# Is It a Match? (working title)

A Tinder-style food swipe game for market research at the MDI fest.

- Round 1: five Chinese twists on Indian dishes (Schezwan Dosa, Noodle Samosa…). Would you try it?
- Round 2: five world cuisines made desi, two sample dishes each. Would you try it?
- Answers: Yes or No. In round 2 each player also gets one movable Desi Crush (saved as "Super Like"). Undo works for the last swipe only.
- About a minute per player: one round switch, no result screens (only the Desi Crush has one).
- Every swipe is saved straight to a Google Sheet.

Edit text, dishes and photo names in `data.js`. Setup steps are in `SETUP.md`.
